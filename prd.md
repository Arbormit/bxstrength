SECTION 2 — COMPLETE UI / UX BLUEPRINT Design Philosophy BxStrength should not feel like a normal gym website. It should feel like a premium UK-based digital coaching platform where every interaction builds trust, professionalism, and motivation. The user should immediately understand: “These people know what they’re doing.” The complete experience should be minimal, elegant, distraction-free and conversion-focused. ⸻ Overall Theme Theme Premium Luxury Professional Scientific Performance Driven Modern Minimal ⸻ Color Theme Primary Matte Black Secondary Charcoal Grey Accent White Supporting Light Grey No bright colours in Phase-1. The feeling should resemble premium brands rather than bodybuilding websites. ⸻ Typography Headings Bold Modern Clean Uppercase Large spacing Body Readable Professional Comfortable line spacing No decorative fonts. ⸻ Home Page Layout Hero Section Full screen.
